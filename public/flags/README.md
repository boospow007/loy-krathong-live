# ASEAN flag

`asean.png` is a local copy of the ASEAN flag used for identification in the country selector and on submitted krathongs. ASEAN has no standardized flag emoji.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ASEAN_Flag.png
Official flag reference: https://asean.org/asean-flag/
The ASEAN flag remains the reserved copyright of ASEAN; it is not covered by the repository's MIT license.

# National flags

The 12 national SVG flags are served locally from the flag-icons project (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/main/flags/4x3), under its MIT license; see LICENSE-flag-icons.txt. Downloaded 2026-11-04. They replace emoji on the selector and krathongs so rendering does not depend on OS flag-emoji support. other.svg is an original neutral globe illustration.
